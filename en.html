Questions about data, cybersecurity and AI

What people ask before we start.

Another question? Book a conversation
Can I hire you for just AI, data or security?

Yes. Each service is available separately: you can start with data, cybersecurity or applied AI. If combining two disciplines would help solve your problem, we will explain why and suggest it. It is never a condition of working together.

How do you decide whether a project is worthwhile?

We start with the problem. During the free consultation, we look at impact, available data and risks, then tell you whether it makes sense to proceed. If it does not, we tell you before charging. You still receive the document with prioritized actions.

How can I automate customer support with AI and stay in control?

We start with recurring questions and build an assistant that answers from your own documents. Someone on your team reviews and approves each response before it is sent. AI saves time on repetitive work while you keep control. We do not deploy AI without human review.

What is a data dashboard, and how can it help my business?

A dashboard shows key business figures, such as sales, payments, inventory or occupancy, and updates from your systems and spreadsheets. For small and medium-sized businesses, it replaces manual reporting and debates about which number is right. Everyone works from the same, current source.

What does a cybersecurity audit include for a small or medium-sized business?

We review what is exposed to the internet, who can access what, shared logins, sensitive data locations and whether backups actually work. You receive a prioritized list of what to fix first and what can wait, with the cost of each step. We agree on the exact scope before starting.

What happens to my data?

We use minimum necessary access and document who can access what before working with your data. Nothing leaves your control without your knowledge, and we do not request data we do not need.

How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

It depends on the problem and scope, so we do not publish a fixed price or generic timeline. We work in stages, with scope and cost agreed before each one begins. During the free consultation, we provide a cost and timeline range for your case. Each stage delivers something you can use.

Do you work with small teams?

Yes. We work with small and medium-sized businesses and independent professionals. Your company size does not determine whether we can help. What matters is a specific problem worth solving.

How do we work together across countries?

Our team is in Argentina and Spain. We work remotely, in English and Spanish, with meetings arranged around your time zone. Before starting, we agree on scope, deliverables, communication channels and access to your systems.

What makes Ynera different from a software agency?

We built CDI, our own functional product, and are developing Agroboeda for a client. You work directly with both partners, from the initial problem through delivery. If the project is not worthwhile, we tell you before charging.

Who will I work with?

Directly with Emiliano and Mariano, the two partners at Ynera. Emiliano handles cybersecurity, software and AI; Mariano handles data science and business. There are no sales layers between your problem and the people solving it. The people who listen during the consultation are the people who design and build.

What happens after delivery?

We can keep operating and improving what we build, as we do with our own products, under a separately agreed maintenance plan. If your team prefers to manage it independently, we provide documentation so it can run without relying on us. That has been our approach from the start.
