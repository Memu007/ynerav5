What people ask before we start.

Another question? Book a conversation
How do you decide whether a project is worthwhile?

During the first conversation, we discuss the problem, its impact and the information available. We offer preliminary guidance and agree on what to review before deciding whether to proceed. Technical assessments and their deliverables are scoped and priced separately.

What happens to my data?

We use minimum necessary access and document who can access what before working with your data. Nothing leaves your control without your knowledge, and we do not request data we do not need.

How much does it cost, and how long does it take?

Cost and timing depend on scope. We first understand the problem and gather the information needed, then propose deliverables, cost and timing. Each stage is agreed before work begins.

How do we work together across countries?

Our team is in Argentina and Spain. We work remotely, in English and Spanish, with meetings arranged around your time zone. Before starting, we agree on scope, deliverables, communication channels and access to your systems.

What happens after delivery?

We can keep operating and improving what we build, as we do with our own products, under a separately agreed maintenance plan. If your team prefers to manage it independently, we provide documentation so it can run without relying on us. That has been our approach from the start.
