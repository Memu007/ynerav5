# Ynera 5 — Current-State Handoff

## Scope

This is the current approved design direction for the next Ynera landing page. It is a handoff blueprint, not an implementation brief or final production art. The source of truth is [YNERA_PAGINA_PERFIL.txt](../../YNERA_PAGINA_PERFIL.txt).

## Approved decisions

- **Audience and positioning:** speak to nontechnical business buyers. Data and AI are Ynera’s main capability; systems bring the intelligence into production; cybersecurity protects the solution before launch.
- **Visual direction:** bright, futuristic, premium, and approachable—not dark, generic, retro-coarse, or childish. Use lightweight layered 2.5D, parallax, and sprites rather than heavy 3D.
- **Spatial concept:** **C2 is selected**: one continuous side-on architectural cutaway. The story must read as one uninterrupted journey, not separated sections.
- **Motion:** scroll progression is smooth and eased, never mechanically frame-locked. The character runs between beats, but slows or stops for meaningful actions. Keep the story legible on mobile and provide a reduced-motion version using the same narrative.

## Approved narrative

1. A Ynera Y-tree is dying; the character notices it and a question mark appears.
2. The character collects relevant data, then runs right to a separate lab.
3. In the lab—**with no tree present**—the character analyzes the data and receives an answer, marked by a restrained exclamation point.
4. The character returns **from the right**, deploys an elegant technological protective perimeter around the tree, then waters or applies the solution.
5. The tree blooms. The character makes a restrained victory jump with a light burst.
6. The page naturally invites the visitor to schedule a meeting.

The business story is: **problem → data → intelligence → protection → solution → result**. After the journey, show services in this order: Data, Artificial Intelligence, systems in production; then projects/cases and the final CTA.

## Adversarial next-step notes

Storytelling and the C2 spatial direction are approved; the art is **not final**. The next art pass must:

- replace any domestic white fence with an elegant deployable technological perimeter;
- make the environment recognizably Ynera rather than generic white-and-blue “AI” imagery;
- give the character a distinctive Ynera silhouette or outfit;
- keep the tree’s Y readable while making it more organic;
- remove generic “Data & AI” labels and excess charts from final art; and
- keep the victory modest.
